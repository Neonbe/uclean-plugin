Files moved to the same-volume Trash generally do not immediately release space. Only unchanged, still-present trashed installer files have an integrated restore operation; partially deleted caches are not a recoverable backup.

Estimated allocated size is not a guaranteed increase in disk availability. Scans have defined scope, access limitations and time/entry budgets. The plugin does not promise to identify all removable data or make an insufficient disk large enough. Keep backups of important files and inspect higher-risk choices.
